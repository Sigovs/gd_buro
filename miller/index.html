The advertised price does not include sales tax, vehicle registration fees, other fees required by law, finance charges and any documentation charges.

If a person writes a check without sufficient funds in an associated account to cover it, the check will bounce, or be returned for insufficient funds. Such state has laws regulating how merchants may respond to bounced checks. In Connecticut, the merchant may file a civil suit and press criminal charges if the check writer does not reimburse them for a bounced check after the merchant has sent several notices regarding the matter.

Merchants and other business owners who accept checks must post a notice where customers are likely to see it warning them of the potential consequences of writing bad checks. The notice must include the civil penalties that bad check writers may also face criminal penalties.
