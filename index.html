◆  Case in focus

A catalogue for two hundred cars.

Inventory rebuilt as a printed record: numbered lots, specification plates, photography given the width it needs. Time-to-enquiry fell because the page stopped selling and started documenting.

[Client 04]  ·  2024

Approach

Facts, given a voice.

A car listing is a record before it is a product. Specification, provenance, mileage, and price are the content — not the small print under a photograph. I set them as a record, so the page reads as evidence rather than as advertising.

Hierarchy comes from space and scale first. Containers, rules, and colour arrive later, and only where space has run out. That is why these pages hold up in grayscale, at thumbnail size, and on the phone screen where most of the traffic actually is.

I build what I design. The prototype you review is the markup that ships, which removes the gap where art direction usually goes to die.
